Tan Viet Coffee Studio

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Tan Viet Coffee Studio is an internal content tool operated by Cà phê Tân Việt (Buôn Ma Thuột, Vietnam). It prepares short marketing videos about our own coffee products and sends them to our own TikTok account so that the account owner can review and post them. The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @pptx.leader.2026.

Privacy Policy

Information we access

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to the account's TikTok inbox/drafts (scope video.upload).

We do not access followers, messages, comments of other users, payment data, or any account other than the connected one.

How we use it

  • To upload videos created by Cà phê Tân Việt to the connected account's drafts, where the owner reviews, edits and decides whether to post.
  • We never post publicly without the owner's action in the TikTok app.

Storage and security

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted by our integration provider and are used only for the uploads described above.
  • Videos and captions are our own marketing content and are stored in our private workspace.
  • We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Tokens are then revoked and deleted. To request deletion of any data, contact phamthiensqlq2@gmail.com.

Children

The tool is not directed to anyone under 18.

Terms of Service

  1. The tool may be used only by the owner of the connected TikTok account, for content about Cà phê Tân Việt products.
  2. All content uploaded must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including labeling AI-generated content.
  3. The tool only uploads drafts; the account owner is responsible for reviewing and publishing each video.
  4. The tool is provided as is, for internal use; we may change or stop it at any time.
  5. Contact: phamthiensqlq2@gmail.com.
